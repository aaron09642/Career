## About Me

I'm Aaron Kiem, an Electrical Engineering student at Arizona State University, expected to graduate in May 2027. My coursework and projects have focused on circuit designs and computations, with hands-on experience in PCB design and microcontroller programming. I'm especially interested in ESP32 programming as it provides wireless connection, and enjoy the process of taking a design from a schematic to a working physical prototype.

Recently, I've worked on projects like the metal detection system for an exploration rover, where I programmed an inductance to digital converter with an LED actuator. And a clap light system, where I designed and built an audio front-end system using an electret microphone and op-amp amplification. These projects sharpened my skills in circuit/PCB designing in KiCAD and programming with MPLab X IDE.

Outside of coursework, I'm study animation with the blender animation software, in addition to some adobe software. In relation to engineering however, I study and am practicing 3D printing from online downloaded prints and self modelled projects. In the future I plan to expand my engineering skills in laser cutting and welding. Currently, I'm looking for opportunities with internships and part/full time positions in manufacturing, electrical integration, and other engineering industries.
